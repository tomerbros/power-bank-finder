Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Paris?

There are 86 power bank rental stations in Paris, run by OneFlash, Juice and voozaa. Stations are mostly in bars, restaurants, event locations and nightclubs. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Paris?

From the published prices: voozaa €4 for an hour, max €10 a day. The cheapest hour is €4 with voozaa. Juice and OneFlash show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Paris?

For an hour, voozaa at €4, going by the published prices; for a whole day, voozaa with a maximum of €10.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Paris?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
