Privacy

Short version: no account, no cookies, and your location stays on your device.

Your location

If you tap "Find stations near me", your browser gives your position to the map so it can show the nearest stations. This happens on your device; we don't receive or store your location.

Visitor statistics

We count visits and clicks with Cloudflare Web Analytics. They don't use cookies and don't track you across websites; we see totals such as pages viewed, countries, devices and which stations are opened, not who you are.

Live power bank counts

When you open a station, the map asks the operator (directly, or through our relay on Cloudflare) how many power banks it has. That request contains the station, not you. Cloudflare, like any web host, briefly processes your IP address to deliver it.

Contact form

If you send a message, we receive your name, email address and message by email, use them only to reply, and delete them when they're no longer needed. The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to block spam.

Other services the map loads

Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap and the map code from unpkg.com; their servers see your IP address when your browser fetches them. Links to Google Maps and operator websites only send you there when you tap them.

Questions

Ask us anything about your data, or ask us to delete a message you sent, through the contact page.
