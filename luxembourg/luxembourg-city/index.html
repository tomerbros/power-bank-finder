Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Luxembourg City?

There are 55 power bank rental stations in Luxembourg City, run by Revive, Chimpy and OneFlash. Stations are mostly in kiosks, restaurants, bars and cafés. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Luxembourg City?

From the published prices: Chimpy €3 for an hour, max €3 a day. The cheapest hour is €3 with Chimpy. OneFlash and Revive show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Luxembourg City?

For an hour, Chimpy at €3, going by the published prices; for a whole day, Chimpy with a maximum of €3.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Luxembourg City?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
