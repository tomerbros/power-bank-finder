About Powerbank Radar

Every power bank rental station on one map, whichever company runs it.

Your phone is about to die and there are three power bank rental companies in town, each with its own app and map. Powerbank Radar puts all of them on one map, so you can see the nearest station, what it costs and whether it's open.

What's on the map

4,981 stations from 17 operators in 8 countries. For each station: the operator, its price, the venue and its opening hours where published, and for 7 operators the number of power banks available right now.

Where the data comes from

From each operator's own public station map or list, refreshed every day. Addresses are completed with PDOK (Netherlands) and OpenStreetMap. Map tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Independent

Powerbank Radar is not affiliated with any of the operators. Prices and stations can change; the operator's app or station is always the final word.
