Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Vienna?

There are 114 power bank rental stations in Vienna, run by Chimpy and voozaa. Stations are mostly in kiosks, bars, entertainment venues and restaurants. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Vienna?

From the published prices: Chimpy €3 for an hour, max €3 a day; voozaa €2–€4 for an hour, max €10 a day. The cheapest hour is €2 with voozaa.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Vienna?

For an hour, voozaa at €2, going by the published prices; for a whole day, Chimpy with a maximum of €3.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Vienna?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
