Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Vienna?

There are 65 power bank rental stations in Vienna, run by Chimpy. Stations are mostly in kiosks, vending machines, hostels and clubs. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Vienna?

From the published prices: Chimpy €3 for an hour, max €3 a day. The cheapest hour is €3 with Chimpy.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Vienna?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
