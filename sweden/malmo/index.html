Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Malmö?

There are 43 power bank rental stations in Malmö, run by LecToGo and OneFlash. Stations are mostly in bars, restaurants, shops and cafés. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Malmö?

From the published prices: LecToGo SEK 29 for an hour, max SEK 99 a day. The cheapest hour is SEK 29 with LecToGo. OneFlash show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Malmö?

For an hour, LecToGo at SEK 29, going by the published prices; for a whole day, LecToGo with a maximum of SEK 99.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Malmö?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
