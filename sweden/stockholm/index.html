Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Stockholm?

There are 313 power bank rental stations in Stockholm, run by LecToGo and OneFlash. Stations are mostly in shops, restaurants, bars and cafés. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Stockholm?

From the published prices: LecToGo SEK 29 for an hour, max SEK 99 a day. The cheapest hour is SEK 29 with LecToGo. OneFlash show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Stockholm?

For an hour, LecToGo at SEK 29, going by the published prices; for a whole day, LecToGo with a maximum of SEK 99.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Stockholm?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
