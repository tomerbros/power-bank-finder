Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Arnhem?

There are 6 power bank rental stations in Arnhem, run by Revive, ICCXX, Juice and Wessview. Stations are mostly in restaurants, cafés and bars. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Arnhem?

Published prices: ICCXX: Max €3 per day; Wessview: €2 / 30 min · max €10 / day. Other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you rent.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Arnhem?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
