Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Rotterdam?

There are 276 power bank rental stations in Rotterdam, run by Buzz, Juice, ICCXX, Revive, AIRVALL, OneFlash and Wessview. Stations are mostly in restaurants, cafés, bars and supermarkets. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Rotterdam?

From the published prices: Buzz €3 for an hour, max €9 a day; AIRVALL €4 for an hour, max €4 a day; Wessview €4 for an hour, max €10 a day. The cheapest hour is €3 with Buzz. ICCXX, Juice, OneFlash and Revive show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Rotterdam?

For an hour, Buzz at €3, going by the published prices; for a whole day, ICCXX with a maximum of €3.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Rotterdam?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
