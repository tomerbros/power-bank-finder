Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Rotterdam?

There are 185 power bank rental stations in Rotterdam, run by Buzz, Juice, ICCXX, Revive, AIRVALL and Wessview. Stations are mostly in restaurants, cafés, supermarkets and bars. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Rotterdam?

Published prices: Buzz: €3 / hour · max €9 / day; ICCXX: Max €3 per day; AIRVALL: €4 per 24 hours; Wessview: €2 / 30 min · max €10 / day. The other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you start.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Rotterdam?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
