Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Den Helder?

There are 8 power bank rental stations in Den Helder, run by ICCXX and Juice. Stations are mostly in entertainment venues, hotels and cafés. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Den Helder?

Published prices: ICCXX: Max €3 per day. The other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you start.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Den Helder?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
