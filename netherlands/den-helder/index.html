Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Den Helder?

There are 11 power bank rental stations in Den Helder, run by ICCXX, Buzz and Juice. Stations are mostly in hotels, entertainment venues and cafés. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Den Helder?

From the published prices: Buzz €3 for an hour, max €9 a day. The cheapest hour is €3 with Buzz. ICCXX and Juice show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Den Helder?

For an hour, Buzz at €3, going by the published prices; for a whole day, ICCXX with a maximum of €3.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Den Helder?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
