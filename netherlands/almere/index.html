Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Almere?

There are 21 power bank rental stations in Almere, run by ICCXX, Juice, Revive, Buzz and Wessview. Stations are mostly in entertainment venues, restaurants, cafés and gyms. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Almere?

Published prices: Buzz: €3 / hour · max €9 / day; ICCXX: Max €3 per day; Wessview: €2 / 30 min · max €10 / day. Other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you rent.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Almere?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
