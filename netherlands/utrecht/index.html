Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Utrecht?

There are 95 power bank rental stations in Utrecht, run by Revive, Juice, ICCXX, Buzz, Amlo, AIRVALL and Wessview. Stations are mostly in restaurants, bars, entertainment venues and cafés. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Utrecht?

Published prices: Amlo: €2.50 / hour; Buzz: €3 / hour · max €9 / day; ICCXX: Max €3 per day; AIRVALL: €4 per 24 hours; Wessview: €2 / 30 min · max €10 / day. The other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you start.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Utrecht?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
