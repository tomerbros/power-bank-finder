Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Utrecht?

There are 97 power bank rental stations in Utrecht, run by Revive, Juice, ICCXX, Buzz, Amlo, AIRVALL, Wessview and OneFlash. Stations are mostly in restaurants, bars, entertainment venues and cafés. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Utrecht?

From the published prices: Buzz €3 for an hour, max €9 a day; Amlo €2.50 for an hour; AIRVALL €4 for an hour, max €4 a day; Wessview €4 for an hour, max €10 a day. The cheapest hour is €2.50 with Amlo. ICCXX, Juice, OneFlash and Revive show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Utrecht?

For an hour, Amlo at €2.50, going by the published prices; for a whole day, ICCXX with a maximum of €3.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Utrecht?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
