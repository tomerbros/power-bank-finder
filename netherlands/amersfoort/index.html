Where can I rent a power bank in Amersfoort?

There are 17 power bank rental stations in Amersfoort, run by Juice, Revive, ICCXX, Buzz and Wessview. Stations are mostly in cafés, restaurants, hotels and schools and universities. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Amersfoort?

Published prices: Buzz: €3 / hour · max €9 / day; ICCXX: Max €3 per day; Wessview: €2 / 30 min · max €10 / day. The other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you start.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Amersfoort?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
