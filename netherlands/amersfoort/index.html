Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Amersfoort?

There are 18 power bank rental stations in Amersfoort, run by Juice, Revive, ICCXX, Buzz, Wessview and OneFlash. Stations are mostly in cafés, restaurants, hotels and schools and universities. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Amersfoort?

From the published prices: Buzz €3 for an hour, max €9 a day; Wessview €4 for an hour, max €10 a day. The cheapest hour is €3 with Buzz. ICCXX, Juice, OneFlash and Revive show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Amersfoort?

For an hour, Buzz at €3, going by the published prices; for a whole day, ICCXX with a maximum of €3.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Amersfoort?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
