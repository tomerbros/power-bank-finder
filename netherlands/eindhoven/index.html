Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Eindhoven?

There are 42 power bank rental stations in Eindhoven, run by Juice, Amlo, ICCXX, Revive and Wessview. Stations are mostly in restaurants, cafés, bars and nightclubs. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Eindhoven?

Published prices: Amlo: €2.50 / hour; ICCXX: Max €3 per day; Wessview: €2 / 30 min · max €10 / day. Other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you rent.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Eindhoven?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
