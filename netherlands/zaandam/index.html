Where can I rent a power bank in Zaandam?

There are 17 power bank rental stations in Zaandam, run by Buzz, ICCXX, Revive and Juice. Stations are mostly in restaurants and hotels. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Zaandam?

Published prices: Buzz: €3 / hour · max €9 / day; ICCXX: Max €3 per day. Other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you rent.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Zaandam?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
