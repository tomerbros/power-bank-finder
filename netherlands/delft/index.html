Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Delft?

There are 13 power bank rental stations in Delft, run by Buzz, ICCXX and Juice. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Delft?

From the published prices: Buzz €3 for an hour, max €9 a day. The cheapest hour is €3 with Buzz. ICCXX and Juice show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Delft?

For an hour, Buzz at €3, going by the published prices; for a whole day, ICCXX with a maximum of €3.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Delft?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
