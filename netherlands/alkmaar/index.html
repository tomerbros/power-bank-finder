Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Alkmaar?

There are 15 power bank rental stations in Alkmaar, run by ICCXX, Juice and Wessview. Stations are mostly in restaurants and cafés. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Alkmaar?

Published prices: ICCXX: Max €3 per day; Wessview: €2 / 30 min · max €10 / day. Other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you rent.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Alkmaar?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
