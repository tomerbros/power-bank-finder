Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Alkmaar?

There are 21 power bank rental stations in Alkmaar, run by ICCXX, Buzz, Juice and Wessview. Stations are mostly in cafés, restaurants and supermarkets. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Alkmaar?

From the published prices: Buzz €3 for an hour, max €9 a day; Wessview €4 for an hour, max €10 a day. The cheapest hour is €3 with Buzz. ICCXX and Juice show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Alkmaar?

For an hour, Buzz at €3, going by the published prices; for a whole day, ICCXX with a maximum of €3.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Alkmaar?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
