Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in The Hague?

There are 143 power bank rental stations in The Hague, run by Juice, Buzz, ICCXX, Revive, AIRVALL and Wessview. Stations are mostly in restaurants, beach clubs, supermarkets and bars. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in The Hague?

Published prices: Buzz: €3 / hour · max €9 / day; ICCXX: Max €3 per day; AIRVALL: €4 per 24 hours; Wessview: €2 / 30 min · max €10 / day. The other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you start.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in The Hague?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
