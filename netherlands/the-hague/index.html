Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in The Hague?

There are 209 power bank rental stations in The Hague, run by Buzz, Juice, ICCXX, Revive, AIRVALL and Wessview. Stations are mostly in restaurants, beach clubs, supermarkets and bars. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in The Hague?

From the published prices: Buzz €3 for an hour, max €9 a day; AIRVALL €4 for an hour, max €4 a day; Wessview €4 for an hour, max €10 a day. The cheapest hour is €3 with Buzz. ICCXX, Juice and Revive show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in The Hague?

For an hour, Buzz at €3, going by the published prices; for a whole day, ICCXX with a maximum of €3.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in The Hague?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
