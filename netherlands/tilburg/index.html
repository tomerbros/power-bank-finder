Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Tilburg?

There are 15 power bank rental stations in Tilburg, run by Juice, ICCXX, Amlo, Revive and Wessview. Stations are mostly in restaurants, cafés, train stations and bars. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Tilburg?

Published prices: Amlo: €2.50 / hour; ICCXX: Max €3 per day; Wessview: €2 / 30 min · max €10 / day. Other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you rent.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Tilburg?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
