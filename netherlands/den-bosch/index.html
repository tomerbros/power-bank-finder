Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Den Bosch?

There are 23 power bank rental stations in Den Bosch, run by Juice, Buzz, ICCXX, Revive, Amlo and Wessview. Stations are mostly in restaurants, bars and phone shops. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Den Bosch?

From the published prices: Buzz €3 for an hour, max €9 a day; Amlo €2.50 for an hour; Wessview €4 for an hour, max €10 a day. The cheapest hour is €2.50 with Amlo. ICCXX, Juice and Revive show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Den Bosch?

For an hour, Amlo at €2.50, going by the published prices; for a whole day, ICCXX with a maximum of €3.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Den Bosch?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
