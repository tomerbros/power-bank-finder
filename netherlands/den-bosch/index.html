Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Den Bosch?

There are 20 power bank rental stations in Den Bosch, run by Juice, ICCXX, Revive, Amlo, Buzz and Wessview. Stations are mostly in restaurants, bars and phone shops. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Den Bosch?

Published prices: Amlo: €2.50 / hour; Buzz: €3 / hour · max €9 / day; ICCXX: Max €3 per day; Wessview: €2 / 30 min · max €10 / day. Other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you rent.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Den Bosch?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
