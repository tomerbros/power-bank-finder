Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Maastricht?

There are 21 power bank rental stations in Maastricht, run by Juice, ICCXX, Revive and Amlo. Stations are mostly in restaurants, cafés, entertainment venues and hotels. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Maastricht?

Published prices: Amlo: €2.50 / hour; ICCXX: Max €3 per day. Other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you rent.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Maastricht?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
