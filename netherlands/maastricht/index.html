Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Maastricht?

There are 22 power bank rental stations in Maastricht, run by Juice, ICCXX, OneFlash, Amlo and Revive. Stations are mostly in restaurants, cafés, entertainment venues and hotels. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Maastricht?

From the published prices: Amlo €2.50 for an hour. The cheapest hour is €2.50 with Amlo. ICCXX, Juice, OneFlash and Revive show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Maastricht?

For an hour, Amlo at €2.50, going by the published prices; for a whole day, ICCXX with a maximum of €3.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Maastricht?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
