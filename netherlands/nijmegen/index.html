Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Nijmegen?

There are 11 power bank rental stations in Nijmegen, run by ICCXX, Revive and Wessview. Stations are mostly in restaurants, schools and universities and entertainment venues. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Nijmegen?

Published prices: ICCXX: Max €3 per day; Wessview: €2 / 30 min · max €10 / day. Other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you rent.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Nijmegen?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
