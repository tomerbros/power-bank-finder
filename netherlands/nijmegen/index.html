Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Nijmegen?

There are 16 power bank rental stations in Nijmegen, run by ICCXX, Buzz, Revive and Wessview. Stations are mostly in restaurants, cafés, schools and universities and entertainment venues. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Nijmegen?

From the published prices: Buzz €3 for an hour, max €9 a day; Wessview €4 for an hour, max €10 a day. The cheapest hour is €3 with Buzz. ICCXX and Revive show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Nijmegen?

For an hour, Buzz at €3, going by the published prices; for a whole day, ICCXX with a maximum of €3.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Nijmegen?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
