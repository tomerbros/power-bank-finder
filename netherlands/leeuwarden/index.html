Where can I rent a power bank in Leeuwarden?

There are 15 power bank rental stations in Leeuwarden, run by ICCXX, Revive and Juice. Stations are mostly in bars, restaurants and cafés. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Leeuwarden?

Published prices: ICCXX: Max €3 per day. Other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you rent.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Leeuwarden?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
