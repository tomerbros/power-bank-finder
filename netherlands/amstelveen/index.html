Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Amstelveen?

There are 23 power bank rental stations in Amstelveen, run by Buzz, Revive and ICCXX. Stations are mostly in restaurants, clubs and bars. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Amstelveen?

From the published prices: Buzz €3 for an hour, max €9 a day. The cheapest hour is €3 with Buzz. ICCXX and Revive show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Amstelveen?

For an hour, Buzz at €3, going by the published prices; for a whole day, ICCXX with a maximum of €3.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Amstelveen?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
