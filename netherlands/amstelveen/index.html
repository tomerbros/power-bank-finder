Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Amstelveen?

There are 21 power bank rental stations in Amstelveen, run by Buzz, Revive and ICCXX. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Amstelveen?

Published prices: Buzz: €3 / hour · max €9 / day; ICCXX: Max €3 per day. The other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you start.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Amstelveen?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
