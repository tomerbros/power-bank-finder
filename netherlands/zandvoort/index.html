Where can I rent a power bank in Zandvoort?

There are 25 power bank rental stations in Zandvoort, run by Juice, ICCXX and Revive. Stations are mostly in beach clubs, restaurants, bars and cafés. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Zandvoort?

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Zandvoort?
