Opening hours and status

25 of the 2,284 stations publish their opening hours; 16 stay open past midnight on at least one day. The map shows whether each one is open right now. On 26 November 2026, 589 stations reported themselves online and 311 offline or closed, and 1,384 don't report a status. On 26 November 2026, the 330 stations that publish their stock had 1,901 power banks ready to rent between them.

How power bank rental works in the Netherlands

Find a station on the map, then rent a power bank with the operator's app or, at some stations, by tapping your bank card. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any station of the same operator. Each operator page explains how it works and what it costs.

Questions

How many power bank rental stations are there in the Netherlands?

Powerbank Radar lists 2,284 stations from 9 operators in the Netherlands (updated 26 November 2026), most of them in Amsterdam, Rotterdam and The Hague.

Which operator has the most stations in the Netherlands?

Buzz, with 876 of the 2,284 stations (38%).

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in the Netherlands?

From the published prices: Buzz €3 for an hour, max €9 a day; Amlo €2.50 for an hour; Wessview €4 for an hour, max €10 a day; AIRVALL €4 for an hour, max €4 a day; ChargeBuddies €2.50 for an hour. The cheapest hour is €2.50 with Amlo. ICCXX, Juice, OneFlash and Revive show the price in the app or on the station.

What is the most I can pay for a day's power bank rental in the Netherlands?

Most operators stop charging at a daily maximum. The lowest in the Netherlands is €3 (ICCXX); see the table for each operator's.
