Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Leiden?

There are 19 power bank rental stations in Leiden, run by Buzz, Juice and Revive. Stations are mostly in restaurants, gyms and cafés. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Leiden?

From the published prices: Buzz €3 for an hour, max €9 a day. The cheapest hour is €3 with Buzz. Juice and Revive show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Leiden?

For an hour, Buzz at €3, going by the published prices; for a whole day, Buzz with a maximum of €9.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Leiden?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
