Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Breda?

There are 21 power bank rental stations in Breda, run by Juice, ICCXX and Amlo. Stations are mostly in restaurants, cafés, nightclubs and bars. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Breda?

Published prices: Amlo: €2.50 / hour; ICCXX: Max €3 per day. The other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you start.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Breda?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
