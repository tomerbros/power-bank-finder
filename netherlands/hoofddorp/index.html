Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Hoofddorp?

There are 15 power bank rental stations in Hoofddorp, run by ICCXX, Revive, Buzz and Juice. Stations are mostly in hotels and restaurants. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Hoofddorp?

Published prices: Buzz: €3 / hour · max €9 / day; ICCXX: Max €3 per day. The other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you start.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Hoofddorp?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
