Where can I rent a power bank in Amsterdam?

There are 666 power bank rental stations in Amsterdam, run by Buzz, Juice, ICCXX, Revive, Wessview and OneFlash. Stations are mostly in restaurants, cafés, hotels and bars. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Amsterdam?

From the published prices: Buzz €3 for an hour, max €9 a day; Wessview €4 for an hour, max €10 a day. The cheapest hour is €3 with Buzz. ICCXX, Juice, OneFlash and Revive show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Amsterdam?

For an hour, Buzz at €3, going by the published prices; for a whole day, ICCXX with a maximum of €3.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Amsterdam?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
