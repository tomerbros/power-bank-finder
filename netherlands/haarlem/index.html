Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Haarlem?

There are 27 power bank rental stations in Haarlem, run by Juice, Buzz, ICCXX and Revive. Stations are mostly in restaurants, supermarkets, hotels and cafés. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Haarlem?

From the published prices: Buzz €3 for an hour, max €9 a day. The cheapest hour is €3 with Buzz. ICCXX, Juice and Revive show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Haarlem?

For an hour, Buzz at €3, going by the published prices; for a whole day, ICCXX with a maximum of €3.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Haarlem?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
