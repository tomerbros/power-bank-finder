Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Hilversum?

There are 8 power bank rental stations in Hilversum, run by Juice, Revive, ICCXX, Amlo and Buzz. Stations are mostly in bars and restaurants. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Hilversum?

Published prices: Amlo: €2.50 / hour; Buzz: €3 / hour · max €9 / day; ICCXX: Max €3 per day. Other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you rent.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Hilversum?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
