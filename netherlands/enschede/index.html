Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Enschede?

There are 23 power bank rental stations in Enschede, run by Buzz, Revive, Juice and ICCXX. Stations are mostly in restaurants and nightclubs. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Enschede?

From the published prices: Buzz €3 for an hour, max €9 a day. The cheapest hour is €3 with Buzz. ICCXX, Juice and Revive show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Enschede?

For an hour, Buzz at €3, going by the published prices; for a whole day, ICCXX with a maximum of €3.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Enschede?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
