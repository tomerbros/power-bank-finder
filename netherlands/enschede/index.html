Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Enschede?

There are 22 power bank rental stations in Enschede, run by Buzz, Revive, Juice and ICCXX. Stations are mostly in restaurants and nightclubs. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Enschede?

Published prices: Buzz: €3 / hour · max €9 / day; ICCXX: Max €3 per day. Other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you rent.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Enschede?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
