Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Zwolle?

There are 20 power bank rental stations in Zwolle, run by Juice, Wessview, Revive, Buzz, ICCXX and OneFlash. Stations are mostly in entertainment venues, restaurants, bars and supermarkets. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Zwolle?

From the published prices: Wessview €4 for an hour, max €10 a day; Buzz €3 for an hour, max €9 a day. The cheapest hour is €3 with Buzz. ICCXX, Juice, OneFlash and Revive show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Zwolle?

For an hour, Buzz at €3, going by the published prices; for a whole day, ICCXX with a maximum of €3.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Zwolle?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
