Where can I rent a power bank in Bellinzona?

There are 25 power bank rental stations in Bellinzona, run by Chimpy. Stations are mostly in vending machines and kiosks. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Bellinzona?

From the published prices: Chimpy CHF 5 for an hour, max CHF 5 a day. The cheapest hour is CHF 5 with Chimpy.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Bellinzona?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
