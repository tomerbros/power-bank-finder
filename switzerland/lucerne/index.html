Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Lucerne?

There are 84 power bank rental stations in Lucerne, run by Chimpy and ANYWATT. Stations are mostly in vending machines, kiosks, bars and entertainment venues. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Lucerne?

From the published prices: Chimpy CHF 5 for an hour, max CHF 5 a day. The cheapest hour is CHF 5 with Chimpy. ANYWATT show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Lucerne?

For an hour, Chimpy at CHF 5, going by the published prices; for a whole day, Chimpy with a maximum of CHF 5.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Lucerne?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
