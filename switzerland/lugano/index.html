Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Lugano?

There are 40 power bank rental stations in Lugano, run by Chimpy and OneFlash. Stations are mostly in vending machines and kiosks. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Lugano?

From the published prices: Chimpy CHF 5 for an hour, max CHF 5 a day. The cheapest hour is CHF 5 with Chimpy. OneFlash show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Lugano?

For an hour, Chimpy at CHF 5, going by the published prices; for a whole day, Chimpy with a maximum of CHF 5.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Lugano?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
