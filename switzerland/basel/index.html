Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Basel?

There are 119 power bank rental stations in Basel, run by Chimpy. Stations are mostly in kiosks, vending machines, hotels and clubs. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Basel?

From the published prices: Chimpy CHF 5 for an hour, max CHF 5 a day. The cheapest hour is CHF 5 with Chimpy.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Basel?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
