Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Baden?

There are 33 power bank rental stations in Baden, run by Chimpy. Stations are mostly in kiosks and vending machines. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Baden?

From the published prices: Chimpy CHF 5 for an hour, max CHF 5 a day. The cheapest hour is CHF 5 with Chimpy.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Baden?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
