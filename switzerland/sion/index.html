Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Sion?

There are 25 power bank rental stations in Sion, run by Chimpy. Stations are mostly in vending machines and kiosks. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Sion?

From the published prices: Chimpy CHF 5 for an hour, max CHF 5 a day. The cheapest hour is CHF 5 with Chimpy.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Sion?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
