Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Zug?

There are 31 power bank rental stations in Zug, run by Chimpy. Stations are mostly in vending machines and kiosks. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Zug?

From the published prices: Chimpy CHF 5 for an hour, max CHF 5 a day. The cheapest hour is CHF 5 with Chimpy.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Zug?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
