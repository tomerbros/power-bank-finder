Where can I rent a power bank in Bern?

There are 159 power bank rental stations in Bern, run by Chimpy. Stations are mostly in vending machines, kiosks and entertainment venues. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Bern?

From the published prices: Chimpy CHF 5 for an hour, max CHF 5 a day. The cheapest hour is CHF 5 with Chimpy.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Bern?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
