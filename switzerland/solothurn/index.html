Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Solothurn?

There are 27 power bank rental stations in Solothurn, run by Chimpy. Stations are mostly in vending machines and kiosks. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Solothurn?

From the published prices: Chimpy CHF 5 for an hour, max CHF 5 a day. The cheapest hour is CHF 5 with Chimpy.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Solothurn?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
