Where can I rent a power bank in Lausanne?

There are 88 power bank rental stations in Lausanne, run by Chimpy. Stations are mostly in vending machines and kiosks. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Lausanne?

From the published prices: Chimpy CHF 5 for an hour, max CHF 5 a day. The cheapest hour is CHF 5 with Chimpy.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Lausanne?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
