Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in St. Gallen?

There are 40 power bank rental stations in St. Gallen, run by Chimpy. Stations are mostly in kiosks, vending machines and entertainment venues. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in St. Gallen?

From the published prices: Chimpy CHF 5 for an hour, max CHF 5 a day. The cheapest hour is CHF 5 with Chimpy.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in St. Gallen?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
