Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Geneva?

There are 115 power bank rental stations in Geneva, run by Chimpy and OneFlash. Stations are mostly in kiosks and vending machines. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Geneva?

From the published prices: Chimpy CHF 5 for an hour, max CHF 5 a day. The cheapest hour is CHF 5 with Chimpy. OneFlash show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Geneva?

For an hour, Chimpy at CHF 5, going by the published prices; for a whole day, Chimpy with a maximum of CHF 5.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Geneva?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
