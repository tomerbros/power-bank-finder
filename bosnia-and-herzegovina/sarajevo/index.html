Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Sarajevo?

There are 41 power bank rental stations in Sarajevo, run by Chargi. Stations are mostly in cafés, restaurants, bars and clubs. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Sarajevo?

From the published prices: Chargi BAM 1–BAM 2.50 for an hour, max BAM 5–BAM 25 a day. The cheapest hour is BAM 1 with Chargi.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Sarajevo?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
