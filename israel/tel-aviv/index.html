Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Tel Aviv?

There are 24 power bank rental stations in Tel Aviv, run by Dead Batt. Stations are mostly in restaurants, cafés and gyms. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Tel Aviv?

Published prices: Dead Batt: ₪15 / hour · max ₪30 / 24 h. Other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you rent.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Tel Aviv?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
