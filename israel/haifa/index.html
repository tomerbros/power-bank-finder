Where can I rent a power bank in Haifa?

There are 18 power bank rental stations in Haifa, run by Dead Batt. Stations are mostly in cafés, bars and restaurants. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Haifa?

Published prices: Dead Batt: ₪15 / hour · max ₪30 / 24 h. Other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you rent.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Haifa?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
