Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Copenhagen?

There are 63 power bank rental stations in Copenhagen, run by LecToGo and OneFlash. Stations are mostly in bars, restaurants, cafés and hotels. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Copenhagen?

From the published prices: LecToGo DKK 29 for an hour, max DKK 99 a day. The cheapest hour is DKK 29 with LecToGo. OneFlash show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Copenhagen?

For an hour, LecToGo at DKK 29, going by the published prices; for a whole day, LecToGo with a maximum of DKK 99.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Copenhagen?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
