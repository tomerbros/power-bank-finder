Questions

Where can I hire a power bank in Reading?

There are 27 power bank hire stations in Reading, run by ChargedUp and Fast Charger. Stations are mostly in bars and restaurants. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does power bank hire cost in Reading?

From the published prices: ChargedUp £4 for an hour, max £12 a day. The cheapest hour is £4 with ChargedUp. Fast Charger show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank hire in Reading?

For an hour, ChargedUp at £4, going by the published prices; for a whole day, ChargedUp with a maximum of £12.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Reading?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
