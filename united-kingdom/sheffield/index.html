Questions

Where can I hire a power bank in Sheffield?

There are 75 power bank hire stations in Sheffield, run by ChargedUp and Fast Charger. Stations are mostly in bars, restaurants, shops and hair salons. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does power bank hire cost in Sheffield?

From the published prices: ChargedUp £4 for an hour, max £12 a day. The cheapest hour is £4 with ChargedUp. Fast Charger show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank hire in Sheffield?

For an hour, ChargedUp at £4, going by the published prices; for a whole day, ChargedUp with a maximum of £12.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Sheffield?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.

Is there a phone charging station near me in Sheffield?

Yes: 75 power bank stations in Sheffield lend you a portable charger to take with you. Open the map and tap "Stations near me" to see the nearest ones and how far they are.
