Questions

Where can I hire a power bank in Bristol?

There are 39 power bank hire stations in Bristol, run by ChargedUp, Fast Charger, Revive and voozaa. Stations are mostly in bars, restaurants, hotels and entertainment venues. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does power bank hire cost in Bristol?

From the published prices: ChargedUp £4 for an hour, max £12 a day; voozaa £4 for an hour, max £10 a day. The cheapest hour is £4 with ChargedUp. Fast Charger and Revive show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank hire in Bristol?

For an hour, ChargedUp at £4, going by the published prices; for a whole day, voozaa with a maximum of £10.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Bristol?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
