Questions

Where can I hire a power bank in Edinburgh?

There are 31 power bank hire stations in Edinburgh, run by ChargedUp and OneFlash. Stations are mostly in bars and restaurants. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does power bank hire cost in Edinburgh?

From the published prices: ChargedUp £4 for an hour, max £12 a day. The cheapest hour is £4 with ChargedUp. OneFlash show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank hire in Edinburgh?

For an hour, ChargedUp at £4, going by the published prices; for a whole day, ChargedUp with a maximum of £12.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Edinburgh?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.

Is there a phone charging station near me in Edinburgh?

Yes: 31 power bank stations in Edinburgh lend you a portable charger to take with you. Open the map and tap "Stations near me" to see the nearest ones and how far they are.
