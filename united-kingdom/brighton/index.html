Questions

Where can I hire a power bank in Brighton?

There are 56 power bank hire stations in Brighton, run by ChargedUp and Fast Charger. Stations are mostly in bars, hotels and restaurants. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does power bank hire cost in Brighton?

From the published prices: ChargedUp £4 for an hour, max £12 a day. The cheapest hour is £4 with ChargedUp. Fast Charger show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank hire in Brighton?

For an hour, ChargedUp at £4, going by the published prices; for a whole day, ChargedUp with a maximum of £12.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Brighton?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.

Is there a phone charging station near me in Brighton?

Yes: 56 power bank stations in Brighton lend you a portable charger to take with you. Open the map and tap "Find stations near me" to see the nearest ones and how far they are.
