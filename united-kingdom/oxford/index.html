Questions

Where can I hire a power bank in Oxford?

There are 21 power bank hire stations in Oxford, run by ChargedUp and Fast Charger. Stations are mostly in bars, cafés and hotels. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does power bank hire cost in Oxford?

From the published prices: ChargedUp £4 for an hour, max £12 a day. The cheapest hour is £4 with ChargedUp. Fast Charger show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank hire in Oxford?

For an hour, ChargedUp at £4, going by the published prices; for a whole day, ChargedUp with a maximum of £12.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Oxford?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
