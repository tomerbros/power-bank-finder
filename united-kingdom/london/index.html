Questions

Where can I hire a power bank in London?

There are 1,044 power bank hire stations in London, run by Fast Charger, ChargedUp, voozaa and Revive. Stations are mostly in bars, restaurants, cafés and entertainment venues. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does power bank hire cost in London?

From the published prices: ChargedUp £4 for an hour, max £12 a day; voozaa £2–£4 for an hour, max £10 a day. The cheapest hour is £2 with voozaa. Fast Charger and Revive show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank hire in London?

For an hour, voozaa at £2, going by the published prices; for a whole day, voozaa with a maximum of £10.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in London?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
