Questions

Where can I hire a power bank in Manchester?

There are 118 power bank hire stations in Manchester, run by ChargedUp and voozaa. Stations are mostly in bars, hotels, restaurants and shopping centres. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does power bank hire cost in Manchester?

From the published prices: ChargedUp £4 for an hour, max £12 a day; voozaa £2 for an hour, max £10 a day. The cheapest hour is £2 with voozaa.

Which is the cheapest power bank hire in Manchester?

For an hour, voozaa at £2, going by the published prices; for a whole day, voozaa with a maximum of £10.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Manchester?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.

Is there a phone charging station near me in Manchester?

Yes: 118 power bank stations in Manchester lend you a portable charger to take with you. Open the map and tap "Find stations near me" to see the nearest ones and how far they are.
