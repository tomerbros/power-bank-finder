Where can I hire a power bank in St Albans?

How much does power bank hire cost in St Albans?

From the published prices: ChargedUp £4 for an hour, max £12 a day. The cheapest hour is £4 with ChargedUp. Fast Charger and Revive show the price in the app or on the station.

For an hour, ChargedUp at £4, going by the published prices; for a whole day, ChargedUp with a maximum of £12.
