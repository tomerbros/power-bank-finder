Do I have to pay a deposit to rent a power bank?

Often. Deposits we found range from €3–€35, held or charged when you start and refunded when you return the power bank. Some operators only pre-authorise your card.

Is there a maximum price per day?

Most operators stop charging at a daily maximum; in euros the ones we found range from €3–€30. Keep the power bank for days and most operators treat it as bought.

How are these prices worked out?

From each operator's published price (FireVolt and Fully publish one per station). We count every started period (half hour or hour) up to the daily maximum and leave out free minutes, so these are upper estimates. The station or app always shows the price before you rent.

Powerbank Radar is independent and not affiliated with any operator. Prices can change; the station or app shows the price before you rent. Spotted a wrong price? Tell us.
