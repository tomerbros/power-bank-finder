Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Bochum?

There are 8 power bank rental stations in Bochum, run by Chimpy and voozaa. Stations are mostly in kiosks, bars, event locations and nightclubs. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Bochum?

From the published prices: Chimpy €4 for an hour, max €4 a day; voozaa €2–€4 for an hour, max €10 a day. The cheapest hour is €2 with voozaa.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Bochum?

For an hour, voozaa at €2, going by the published prices; for a whole day, Chimpy with a maximum of €4.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Bochum?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
