Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Stuttgart?

There are 17 power bank rental stations in Stuttgart, run by Chimpy, voozaa and Rechargy. Stations are mostly in kiosks, bars, restaurants and hotels. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Stuttgart?

From the published prices: Chimpy €4 for an hour, max €4 a day; voozaa €2–€4 for an hour, max €10 a day. The cheapest hour is €2 with voozaa. Rechargy show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Stuttgart?

For an hour, voozaa at €2, going by the published prices; for a whole day, Chimpy with a maximum of €4.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Stuttgart?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
