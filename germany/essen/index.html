Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Essen?

There are 22 power bank rental stations in Essen, run by Rechargy and Chimpy. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Essen?

From the published prices: Chimpy €4 for an hour, max €4 a day. The cheapest hour is €4 with Chimpy. Rechargy show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Essen?

For an hour, Chimpy at €4, going by the published prices; for a whole day, Chimpy with a maximum of €4.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Essen?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
