Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Cologne?

There are 58 power bank rental stations in Cologne, run by voozaa and Chimpy. Stations are mostly in restaurants, kiosks, entertainment venues and hotels. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Cologne?

From the published prices: voozaa €2–€4 for an hour, max €10 a day; Chimpy €4 for an hour, max €4 a day. The cheapest hour is €2 with voozaa.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Cologne?

For an hour, voozaa at €2, going by the published prices; for a whole day, Chimpy with a maximum of €4.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Cologne?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
