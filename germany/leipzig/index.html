Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Leipzig?

There are 55 power bank rental stations in Leipzig, run by voozaa and Chimpy. Stations are mostly in restaurants, kiosks, bars and nightclubs. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Leipzig?

From the published prices: voozaa €2–€4 for an hour, max €10 a day; Chimpy €4 for an hour, max €4 a day. The cheapest hour is €2 with voozaa.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Leipzig?

For an hour, voozaa at €2, going by the published prices; for a whole day, Chimpy with a maximum of €4.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Leipzig?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
