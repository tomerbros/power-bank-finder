Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Frankfurt?

There are 29 power bank rental stations in Frankfurt, run by voozaa, Rechargy and Chimpy. Stations are mostly in bars, kiosks, hotels and event locations. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Frankfurt?

From the published prices: voozaa €2–€4 for an hour, max €10 a day; Chimpy €4 for an hour, max €4 a day. The cheapest hour is €2 with voozaa. Rechargy show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Frankfurt?

For an hour, voozaa at €2, going by the published prices; for a whole day, Chimpy with a maximum of €4.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Frankfurt?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
