Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Berlin?

There are 286 power bank rental stations in Berlin, run by Rechargy, voozaa and Chimpy. Stations are mostly in kiosks, bars, restaurants and entertainment venues. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Berlin?

From the published prices: voozaa €2–€4 for an hour, max €10 a day; Chimpy €4 for an hour, max €4 a day. The cheapest hour is €2 with voozaa. Rechargy show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Berlin?

For an hour, voozaa at €2, going by the published prices; for a whole day, Chimpy with a maximum of €4.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Berlin?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
