Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Munich?

There are 61 power bank rental stations in Munich, run by voozaa, Chimpy and Rechargy. Stations are mostly in restaurants, kiosks, bars and entertainment venues. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Munich?

From the published prices: voozaa €2–€4 for an hour, max €10 a day; Chimpy €4 for an hour, max €4 a day. The cheapest hour is €2 with voozaa. Rechargy show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Munich?

For an hour, voozaa at €2, going by the published prices; for a whole day, Chimpy with a maximum of €4.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Munich?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
