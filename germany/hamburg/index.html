Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Hamburg?

There are 111 power bank rental stations in Hamburg, run by Chimpy, voozaa, Rechargy and OneFlash. Stations are mostly in kiosks, bars, restaurants and cafés. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Hamburg?

From the published prices: Chimpy €4 for an hour, max €4 a day; voozaa €2–€4 for an hour, max €10 a day. The cheapest hour is €2 with voozaa. OneFlash and Rechargy show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Hamburg?

For an hour, voozaa at €2, going by the published prices; for a whole day, Chimpy with a maximum of €4.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Hamburg?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
