Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Hanover?

There are 6 power bank rental stations in Hanover, run by voozaa and Chimpy. Stations are mostly in restaurants, vending machines, bars and kiosks. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Hanover?

From the published prices: voozaa €2–€4 for an hour, max €10 a day; Chimpy €4 for an hour, max €4 a day. The cheapest hour is €2 with voozaa.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Hanover?

For an hour, voozaa at €2, going by the published prices; for a whole day, Chimpy with a maximum of €4.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Hanover?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
