Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Karlsruhe?

There are 17 power bank rental stations in Karlsruhe, run by voozaa, Juice, Rechargy and Chimpy. Stations are mostly in restaurants, entertainment venues, gyms and nightclubs. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Karlsruhe?

From the published prices: voozaa €2–€4 for an hour, max €10 a day; Chimpy €4 for an hour, max €4 a day. The cheapest hour is €2 with voozaa. Juice and Rechargy show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Karlsruhe?

For an hour, voozaa at €2, going by the published prices; for a whole day, Chimpy with a maximum of €4.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Karlsruhe?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
