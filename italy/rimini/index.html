Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Rimini?

There are 11 power bank rental stations in Rimini, run by Nomopower, Mobbi and voozaa. Stations are mostly in bars and beach clubs. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Rimini?

From the published prices: Mobbi €2 for an hour; voozaa €4 for an hour, max €10 a day. The cheapest hour is €2 with Mobbi. Nomopower show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Rimini?

For an hour, Mobbi at €2, going by the published prices; for a whole day, voozaa with a maximum of €10.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Rimini?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
