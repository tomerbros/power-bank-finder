Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Rome?

There are 60 power bank rental stations in Rome, run by Recharge.me, Mobbi, voozaa and OneFlash. Stations are mostly in restaurants, cafés, bars and hotels. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Rome?

From the published prices: Mobbi €2 for an hour; voozaa €2–€4 for an hour, max €10 a day. The cheapest hour is €2 with Mobbi. OneFlash and Recharge.me show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Rome?

For an hour, Mobbi at €2, going by the published prices; for a whole day, voozaa with a maximum of €10.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Rome?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
