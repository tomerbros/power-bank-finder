Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Rome?

There are 51 power bank rental stations in Rome, run by Recharge.me and Mobbi. Stations are mostly in restaurants, cafés, bars and hotels. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Rome?

Published prices: Mobbi: €1 / 30 min. Other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you rent.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Rome?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
