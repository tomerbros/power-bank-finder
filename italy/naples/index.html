Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Naples?

There are 33 power bank rental stations in Naples, run by voozaa and Mobbi. Stations are mostly in restaurants, bars, entertainment venues and cafés. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Naples?

From the published prices: voozaa €2–€4 for an hour, max €10 a day; Mobbi €2 for an hour. The cheapest hour is €2 with Mobbi.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Naples?

For an hour, Mobbi at €2, going by the published prices; for a whole day, voozaa with a maximum of €10.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Naples?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
