Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Palermo?

There are 26 power bank rental stations in Palermo, run by Mobbi and voozaa. Stations are mostly in entertainment venues, bars, cafés and hotels. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Palermo?

From the published prices: Mobbi €2 for an hour; voozaa €2–€4 for an hour, max €10 a day. The cheapest hour is €2 with Mobbi.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Palermo?

For an hour, Mobbi at €2, going by the published prices; for a whole day, voozaa with a maximum of €10.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Palermo?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
