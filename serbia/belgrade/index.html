Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Belgrade?

There are 171 power bank rental stations in Belgrade, run by Dopuni.se. Stations are mostly in bars, cafés, hotels and shopping centres. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Belgrade?

From the published prices: Dopuni.se RSD 120 for an hour, max RSD 500 a day. The cheapest hour is RSD 120 with Dopuni.se.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Belgrade?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
