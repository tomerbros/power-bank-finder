Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Kraków?

There are 13 power bank rental stations in Kraków, run by W KONTAKCIE and Fast Energy. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Kraków?

From the published prices: W KONTAKCIE PLN 6 for an hour. The cheapest hour is PLN 6 with W KONTAKCIE. Fast Energy show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Kraków?

For an hour, W KONTAKCIE at PLN 6, going by the published prices.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Kraków?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
