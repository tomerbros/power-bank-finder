Where can I rent a power bank in Nicosia?

There are 98 power bank rental stations in Nicosia, run by WE53. Stations are mostly in cafés, bars, restaurants and gyms. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Nicosia?

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Nicosia?
