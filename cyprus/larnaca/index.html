Where can I rent a power bank in Larnaca?

There are 36 power bank rental stations in Larnaca, run by WE53. Stations are mostly in hotels, bars, cafés and shopping centres. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Larnaca?

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Larnaca?
