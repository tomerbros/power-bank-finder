Where can I rent a power bank in Lviv?

There are 79 power bank rental stations in Lviv, run by Fast Energy. Stations are mostly in hotels, restaurants, bars and cafés. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Lviv?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.
