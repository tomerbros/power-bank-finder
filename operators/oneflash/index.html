Questions

Where can I return a OneFlash power bank?

At any OneFlash station. OneFlash has 325 stations in France, the Netherlands, Spain, Italy, Germany, Switzerland, the UK, Portugal, Luxembourg and Austria, the most in Paris (61). You can't return it to another operator's station.

How much does it cost to rent a OneFlash power bank?

Usually €2–3 per session or hour. Price shown on the station before you pay · free at some venues (e.g. IKEA).

Can I see if a OneFlash station has power banks available?

Not on Powerbank Radar: OneFlash doesn't publish availability. The station or app shows it on the spot.

How big is OneFlash in France?

OneFlash runs 285 of the 324 power bank stations on our map in France (88%), making it number 1 of 3 operators there.

How to rent

Find a OneFlash station on the map, then follow the steps on the station's screen or in the OneFlash app to take a power bank. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any OneFlash station when you're done.

Powerbank Radar is not affiliated with OneFlash. For help with a rental, contact OneFlash directly.
