Questions

Where can I return a Mobbi power bank?

At any Mobbi station. Mobbi has 336 stations in Italy and Spain, the most in Milan (161). You can't return it to another operator's station.

How much does it cost to rent a Mobbi power bank?

€1 / 30 min. First 3 min free · rent by scanning the QR code.

Can I see if a Mobbi station has power banks available?

Yes. When you open a Mobbi station on the map, Powerbank Radar asks Mobbi for the number of power banks ready to rent right now.

How big is Mobbi in Italy?

Mobbi runs 329 of the 368 power bank stations on our map in Italy (89%), making it number 1 of 3 operators there.

How to rent

Find a Mobbi station on the map, then follow the steps on the station's screen or in the Mobbi app to take a power bank. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any Mobbi station when you're done.

Powerbank Radar is not affiliated with Mobbi. For help with a rental, contact Mobbi directly.
