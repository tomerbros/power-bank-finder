Questions

Where can I return a Nero Charge power bank?

At any Nero Charge station. Nero Charge has 41 stations in Belgium, the most in Antwerp (34). You can't return it to another operator's station.

How much does it cost to rent a Nero Charge power bank?

Nero Charge doesn't publish one price: small hourly fee, shown on the station screen. No app: tap your card or phone on the station · deposit pre-authorised, released on return (charged if not back within 48 h) · return at any Nero station.

Can I see if a Nero Charge station has power banks available?

Not on Powerbank Radar: Nero Charge doesn't publish availability. The station or app shows it on the spot.

How big is Nero Charge in Belgium?

Nero Charge runs 41 of the 42 power bank stations on our map in Belgium (98%), making it number 1 of 2 operators there.

How to rent

Find a Nero Charge station on the map, then follow the steps on the station's screen or in the Nero Charge app to take a power bank. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any Nero Charge station when you're done.

Powerbank Radar is not affiliated with Nero Charge. For help with a rental, contact Nero Charge directly.
