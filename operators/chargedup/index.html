Questions

Where can I return a ChargedUp power bank?

At any ChargedUp station. ChargedUp has 3,828 stations in the UK, the most in London (511). You can't return it to another operator's station.

How much does it cost to rent a ChargedUp power bank?

£2 / 30 min · max £12 / day. First 5 min free · rent with the ChargedUp app, return at any ChargedUp station. Across its stations, an hour comes to £4 and three hours to £12, with a daily maximum of £12.

Can I see if a ChargedUp station has power banks available?

Not on Powerbank Radar: ChargedUp doesn't publish availability. The station or app shows it on the spot.

How big is ChargedUp in the UK?

ChargedUp runs 3,828 of the 4,469 power bank stations on our map in the UK (86%), making it number 1 of 5 operators there.

How to rent

Find a ChargedUp station on the map, then follow the steps on the station's screen or in the ChargedUp app to take a power bank. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any ChargedUp station when you're done.

Powerbank Radar is not affiliated with ChargedUp. For help with a rental, contact ChargedUp directly.
