Questions

Where can I return a Fast Charger power bank?

At any Fast Charger station. Fast Charger has 604 stations in the UK, the most in London (516). You can't return it to another operator's station.

How much does it cost to rent a Fast Charger power bank?

Fast Charger doesn't publish one price: price shown in the fast charger app. Rent by scanning the station with the app.

Can I see if a Fast Charger station has power banks available?

Not on Powerbank Radar: Fast Charger doesn't publish availability. The station or app shows it on the spot.

How big is Fast Charger in the UK?

Fast Charger runs 604 of the 4,469 power bank stations on our map in the UK (14%), making it number 2 of 5 operators there.

How to rent

Find a Fast Charger station on the map, then follow the steps on the station's screen or in the Fast Charger app to take a power bank. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any Fast Charger station when you're done.

Powerbank Radar is not affiliated with Fast Charger. For help with a rental, contact Fast Charger directly.
