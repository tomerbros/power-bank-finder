Questions

Where can I return a W KONTAKCIE power bank?

At any W KONTAKCIE station. W KONTAKCIE has 12 stations in Poland, the most in Kraków (10). You can't return it to another operator's station.

How much does it cost to rent a W KONTAKCIE power bank?

From PLN 6 / hour. No app needed to rent · Type-C, Lightning and micro-USB cables included · full price list in their FAQ. Across its stations, an hour comes to PLN 6 and three hours to PLN 18.

Can I see if a W KONTAKCIE station has power banks available?

Not on Powerbank Radar: W KONTAKCIE doesn't publish availability. The station or app shows it on the spot.

How big is W KONTAKCIE in Poland?

W KONTAKCIE runs 12 of the 103 power bank stations on our map in Poland (12%), making it number 2 of 2 operators there.

How to rent

Find a W KONTAKCIE station on the map, then follow the steps on the station's screen or in the W KONTAKCIE app to take a power bank. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any W KONTAKCIE station when you're done.

Powerbank Radar is not affiliated with W KONTAKCIE. For help with a rental, contact W KONTAKCIE directly.
