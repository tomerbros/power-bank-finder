Questions

Where can I return a Amlo power bank?

At any Amlo station. Amlo has 26 stations in the Netherlands, the most in Eindhoven (11). You can't return it to another operator's station.

How much does it cost to rent a Amlo power bank?

€2.50 / hour. Rent by scanning the QR code on the station. Across its stations, an hour comes to €2.50 and three hours to €7.50.

Can I see if a Amlo station has power banks available?

Yes. When you open a Amlo station on the map, Powerbank Radar asks Amlo for the number of power banks ready to rent right now.

How big is Amlo in the Netherlands?

Amlo runs 26 of the 1,897 power bank stations on our map in the Netherlands (1%), making it number 5 of 8 operators there.

How to rent

Find a Amlo station on the map, then follow the steps on the station's screen or in the Amlo app to take a power bank. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any Amlo station when you're done.

Powerbank Radar is not affiliated with Amlo. For help with a rental, contact Amlo directly.
