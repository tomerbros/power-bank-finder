Questions

Where can I return a E-bee power bank?

At any E-bee station. E-bee has 4 stations in Lithuania, the most in Vilnius (2). You can't return it to another operator's station.

How much does it cost to rent a E-bee power bank?

€1.49 / hour. First 30 min free on your first rental · rent with the E-bee app, return at any E-bee station · shown here: the venues E-bee features on its website, more in the app. Across its stations, an hour comes to €1.49 and three hours to €4.47.

Can I see if a E-bee station has power banks available?

Not on Powerbank Radar: E-bee doesn't publish availability. The station or app shows it on the spot.

How big is E-bee in Lithuania?

E-bee runs 4 of the 4 power bank stations on our map in Lithuania (100%), making it number 1 of 1 operators there.

How to rent

Find a E-bee station on the map, then follow the steps on the station's screen or in the E-bee app to take a power bank. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any E-bee station when you're done.

Powerbank Radar is not affiliated with E-bee. For help with a rental, contact E-bee directly.
