Questions

Where can I return a Nomopower power bank?

At any Nomopower station. Nomopower has 51 stations in Italy and San Marino, the most in Rimini (6). You can't return it to another operator's station.

How much does it cost to rent a Nomopower power bank?

Nomopower doesn't publish one price: price shown at the station or in the app. Pay with your card or phone at the station · deposit held, the rental is deducted and the rest refunded on return · USB-C, Lightning and micro-USB cables.

Can I see if a Nomopower station has power banks available?

Not on Powerbank Radar: Nomopower doesn't publish availability. The station or app shows it on the spot.

How big is Nomopower in Italy?

Nomopower runs 50 of the 667 power bank stations on our map in Italy (7%), making it number 3 of 9 operators there.

How to rent

Find a Nomopower station on the map, then follow the steps on the station's screen or in the Nomopower app to take a power bank. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any Nomopower station when you're done.

Powerbank Radar is not affiliated with Nomopower. For help with a rental, contact Nomopower directly.
