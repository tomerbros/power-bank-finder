Questions

Where can I return a FireVolt power bank?

At any FireVolt station. FireVolt has 1,048 stations in Spain, the most in Valencia (200). You can't return it to another operator's station.

How much does it cost to rent a FireVolt power bank?

FireVolt doesn't publish one price: price varies per station. Shown on the station before you rent. Across its stations, an hour comes to €1.90–€6 and three hours to €5–€18, with a daily maximum of €5–€30.

Can I see if a FireVolt station has power banks available?

Yes. When you open a FireVolt station on the map, Powerbank Radar asks FireVolt for the number of power banks ready to rent right now.

How big is FireVolt in Spain?

FireVolt runs 1,048 of the 1,960 power bank stations on our map in Spain (53%), making it number 1 of 11 operators there.

How to rent

Find a FireVolt station on the map, then follow the steps on the station's screen or in the FireVolt app to take a power bank. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any FireVolt station when you're done.

Powerbank Radar is not affiliated with FireVolt. For help with a rental, contact FireVolt directly.
