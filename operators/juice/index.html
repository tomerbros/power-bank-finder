Questions

Where can I return a Juice power bank?

At any Juice station. Juice has 561 stations in the Netherlands, France and Germany, the most in Amsterdam (92). You can't return it to another operator's station.

How much does it cost to rent a Juice power bank?

Juice doesn't publish one price: price varies per location. Shown on the station screen before you rent · deposit held on your card.

Can I see if a Juice station has power banks available?

Yes. When you open a Juice station on the map, Powerbank Radar asks Juice for the number of power banks ready to rent right now.

How big is Juice in the Netherlands?

Juice runs 523 of the 1,897 power bank stations on our map in the Netherlands (28%), making it number 1 of 8 operators there.

How to rent

Find a Juice station on the map, then follow the steps on the station's screen or in the Juice app to take a power bank. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any Juice station when you're done.

Powerbank Radar is not affiliated with Juice. For help with a rental, contact Juice directly.
