Questions

Where can I return a Dead Batt power bank?

At any Dead Batt station. Dead Batt has 156 stations in Israel, the most in Tel Aviv (24). You can't return it to another operator's station.

How much does it cost to rent a Dead Batt power bank?

₪15 / hour · max ₪30 / 24 h. No app: tap your card or phone on the station · return at any Dead Batt station. Across its stations, an hour comes to ₪15 and three hours to ₪30, with a daily maximum of ₪30.

Can I see if a Dead Batt station has power banks available?

Not on Powerbank Radar: Dead Batt doesn't publish availability. The station or app shows it on the spot.

How big is Dead Batt in Israel?

Dead Batt runs 156 of the 156 power bank stations on our map in Israel (100%), making it number 1 of 1 operators there.

How to rent

Find a Dead Batt station on the map, then follow the steps on the station's screen or in the Dead Batt app to take a power bank. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any Dead Batt station when you're done.

Powerbank Radar is not affiliated with Dead Batt. For help with a rental, contact Dead Batt directly.
