Questions

Where can I return a VolterGy power bank?

At any VolterGy station. VolterGy has 55 stations in Spain, the most in Santander (32). You can't return it to another operator's station.

How much does it cost to rent a VolterGy power bank?

€1.75 / 30 min · max €8.75 / day. First 5 min free · €19 deposit, refunded within 48 h.

Can I see if a VolterGy station has power banks available?

Not on Powerbank Radar: VolterGy doesn't publish availability. The station or app shows it on the spot.

How big is VolterGy in Spain?

VolterGy runs 55 of the 1,651 power bank stations on our map in Spain (3%), making it number 4 of 7 operators there.

How to rent

Find a VolterGy station on the map, then follow the steps on the station's screen or in the VolterGy app to take a power bank. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any VolterGy station when you're done.

Powerbank Radar is not affiliated with VolterGy. For help with a rental, contact VolterGy directly.
