Questions

Where can I return a Rechargy power bank?

At any Rechargy station. Rechargy has 252 stations in Germany, the most in Berlin (172). You can't return it to another operator's station.

How much does it cost to rent a Rechargy power bank?

Rechargy doesn't publish one price: price shown in the app or at the terminal. Rent with a QR code · return at any Rechargy station.

Can I see if a Rechargy station has power banks available?

Not on Powerbank Radar: Rechargy doesn't publish availability. The station or app shows it on the spot.

How big is Rechargy in Germany?

Rechargy runs 252 of the 257 power bank stations on our map in Germany (98%), making it number 1 of 2 operators there.

How to rent

Find a Rechargy station on the map, then follow the steps on the station's screen or in the Rechargy app to take a power bank. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any Rechargy station when you're done.

Powerbank Radar is not affiliated with Rechargy. For help with a rental, contact Rechargy directly.
