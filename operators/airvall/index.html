Questions

Where can I return a AIRVALL power bank?

At any AIRVALL station. AIRVALL has 13 stations in the Netherlands, the most in The Hague (4). You can't return it to another operator's station.

How much does it cost to rent a AIRVALL power bank?

€4 per 24 hours. No hourly rate · no deposit in the app, deposit when paying by card.

Can I see if a AIRVALL station has power banks available?

Not on Powerbank Radar: AIRVALL doesn't publish availability. The station or app shows it on the spot.

How big is AIRVALL in the Netherlands?

AIRVALL runs 13 of the 1,895 power bank stations on our map in the Netherlands (1%), making it number 7 of 8 operators there.

How to rent

Find a AIRVALL station on the map, then follow the steps on the station's screen or in the AIRVALL app to take a power bank. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any AIRVALL station when you're done.

Powerbank Radar is not affiliated with AIRVALL. For help with a rental, contact AIRVALL directly.
