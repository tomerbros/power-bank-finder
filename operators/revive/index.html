Questions

Where can I return a Revive power bank?

At any Revive station. Revive has 766 stations in Spain, the Netherlands, Luxembourg and the UK, the most in Ibiza (114). You can't return it to another operator's station.

How much does it cost to rent a Revive power bank?

Revive doesn't publish one price: price shown in the revive app. Billed per started half hour.

Can I see if a Revive station has power banks available?

Not on Powerbank Radar: Revive doesn't publish availability. The station or app shows it on the spot.

How big is Revive in Spain?

Revive runs 381 of the 1,932 power bank stations on our map in Spain (20%), making it number 2 of 10 operators there.

How to rent

Find a Revive station on the map, then follow the steps on the station's screen or in the Revive app to take a power bank. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any Revive station when you're done.

Powerbank Radar is not affiliated with Revive. For help with a rental, contact Revive directly.
