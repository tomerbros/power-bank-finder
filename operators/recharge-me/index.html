Questions

Where can I return a Recharge.me power bank?

At any Recharge.me station. Recharge.me has 37 stations in Italy, the most in Rome (35). You can't return it to another operator's station.

How much does it cost to rent a Recharge.me power bank?

Recharge.me doesn't publish one price: price shown on the station screen. Pay only for the time you use.

Can I see if a Recharge.me station has power banks available?

Yes. When you open a Recharge.me station on the map, Powerbank Radar asks Recharge.me for the number of power banks ready to rent right now.

How big is Recharge.me in Italy?

Recharge.me runs 37 of the 368 power bank stations on our map in Italy (10%), making it number 2 of 3 operators there.

How to rent

Find a Recharge.me station on the map, then follow the steps on the station's screen or in the Recharge.me app to take a power bank. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any Recharge.me station when you're done.

Powerbank Radar is not affiliated with Recharge.me. For help with a rental, contact Recharge.me directly.
