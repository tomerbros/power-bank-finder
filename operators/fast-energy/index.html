Questions

Where can I return a Fast Energy power bank?

At any Fast Energy station. Fast Energy has 131 stations in Poland and Italy, the most in Wrocław (22). You can't return it to another operator's station.

How much does it cost to rent a Fast Energy power bank?

Fast Energy doesn't publish one price: price shown in the fast energy app. Rent with the Fast Energy app · return at any Fast Energy station.

Can I see if a Fast Energy station has power banks available?

Not on Powerbank Radar: Fast Energy doesn't publish availability. The station or app shows it on the spot.

How big is Fast Energy in Poland?

Fast Energy runs 91 of the 103 power bank stations on our map in Poland (88%), making it number 1 of 2 operators there.

How to rent

Find a Fast Energy station on the map, then follow the steps on the station's screen or in the Fast Energy app to take a power bank. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any Fast Energy station when you're done.

Powerbank Radar is not affiliated with Fast Energy. For help with a rental, contact Fast Energy directly.
