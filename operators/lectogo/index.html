Questions

Where can I return a LecToGo power bank?

At any LecToGo station. LecToGo has 1,690 stations in Sweden, Finland, Denmark and Norway, the most in Helsinki (316). You can't return it to another operator's station.

How much does it cost to rent a LecToGo power bank?

SEK 29 / 2 h in the app · max SEK 99 / day. SEK 39 / 2 h without the app (scan the QR code, pay in the browser) · cables included · return at any LecToGo station. Across its stations, an hour comes to SEK 2.95–SEK 39 and three hours to SEK 5.90–SEK 78, with a daily maximum of SEK 8–SEK 99.

Can I see if a LecToGo station has power banks available?

Not on Powerbank Radar: LecToGo doesn't publish availability. The station or app shows it on the spot.

How big is LecToGo in Sweden?

LecToGo runs 802 of the 807 power bank stations on our map in Sweden (99%), making it number 1 of 2 operators there.

How to rent

Find a LecToGo station on the map, then follow the steps on the station's screen or in the LecToGo app to take a power bank. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any LecToGo station when you're done.

Powerbank Radar is not affiliated with LecToGo. For help with a rental, contact LecToGo directly.
