Questions

Where can I return a Chimpy power bank?

At any Chimpy station. Chimpy has 4,288 stations in Switzerland, Germany, Austria, Luxembourg and Italy, the most in Zurich (466). You can't return it to another operator's station.

How much does it cost to rent a Chimpy power bank?

CHF 5 per rental, keep it up to 7 days. CHF 15 deposit, refunded when you return it · no app: buy it at the kiosk checkout, return at any Chimpy point. Across its stations, an hour comes to CHF 3–CHF 5 and three hours to CHF 3–CHF 5, with a daily maximum of CHF 3–CHF 5.

Can I see if a Chimpy station has power banks available?

Not on Powerbank Radar: Chimpy doesn't publish availability. The station or app shows it on the spot.

How big is Chimpy in Switzerland?

Chimpy runs 3,784 of the 3,790 power bank stations on our map in Switzerland (100%), making it number 1 of 3 operators there.

How to rent

Find a Chimpy station on the map, then follow the steps on the station's screen or in the Chimpy app to take a power bank. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any Chimpy station when you're done.

Powerbank Radar is not affiliated with Chimpy. For help with a rental, contact Chimpy directly.
