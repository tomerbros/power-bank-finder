Questions

Where can I return a PowerBrick power bank?

At any PowerBrick station. PowerBrick has 11 stations in Croatia, the most in Zagreb (11). You can't return it to another operator's station.

How much does it cost to rent a PowerBrick power bank?

€3 per 24 hours. First 5 min free · return at any PowerBrick base within 24 hours · rent with the PowerBrick app. Across its stations, an hour comes to €3 and three hours to €3, with a daily maximum of €3.

Can I see if a PowerBrick station has power banks available?

Not on Powerbank Radar: PowerBrick doesn't publish availability. The station or app shows it on the spot.

How big is PowerBrick in Croatia?

PowerBrick runs 11 of the 11 power bank stations on our map in Croatia (100%), making it number 1 of 1 operators there.

How to rent

Find a PowerBrick station on the map, then follow the steps on the station's screen or in the PowerBrick app to take a power bank. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any PowerBrick station when you're done.

Powerbank Radar is not affiliated with PowerBrick. For help with a rental, contact PowerBrick directly.
