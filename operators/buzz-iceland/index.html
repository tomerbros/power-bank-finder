Questions

Where can I return a Buzz Iceland power bank?

At any Buzz Iceland station. Buzz Iceland has 32 stations in Iceland, the most in Reykjavík (31). You can't return it to another operator's station.

How much does it cost to rent a Buzz Iceland power bank?

Buzz Iceland doesn't publish one price: start fee + per-minute rate, shown in the app before you rent. Max ISK 1,500 for the first 24 hours · not returned within 24 h: ISK 3,000 (ISK 10,000 after 72 h, then the power bank is yours) · rent with the Buzz app.

Can I see if a Buzz Iceland station has power banks available?

Not on Powerbank Radar: Buzz Iceland doesn't publish availability. The station or app shows it on the spot.

How big is Buzz Iceland in Iceland?

Buzz Iceland runs 32 of the 32 power bank stations on our map in Iceland (100%), making it number 1 of 1 operators there.

How to rent

Find a Buzz Iceland station on the map, then follow the steps on the station's screen or in the Buzz Iceland app to take a power bank. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any Buzz Iceland station when you're done.

Powerbank Radar is not affiliated with Buzz Iceland. For help with a rental, contact Buzz Iceland directly.
