Where can I rent a power bank in Santa Cruz de Tenerife?

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Santa Cruz de Tenerife?

Where can I charge my phone in Santa Cruz de Tenerife?

At any of the 39 power bank rental stations in Santa Cruz de Tenerife: rent a portable charger, keep using your phone while it charges and return it later. Tap "Find stations near me" on the map to see the nearest ones.

Can tourists rent a power bank in Santa Cruz de Tenerife?
