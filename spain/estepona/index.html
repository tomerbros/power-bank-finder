Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Estepona?

There are 36 power bank rental stations in Estepona, run by Revive, FireVolt and voozaa. Stations are mostly in restaurants, hotels, bars and beach clubs. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Estepona?

From the published prices: FireVolt €4–€6 for an hour, max €10–€30 a day; voozaa €2–€4 for an hour, max €10 a day. The cheapest hour is €2 with voozaa. Revive show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Estepona?

For an hour, voozaa at €2, going by the published prices; for a whole day, FireVolt with a maximum of €10.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Estepona?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.

Where can I charge my phone in Estepona?

At any of the 36 power bank rental stations in Estepona: rent a portable charger, keep using your phone while it charges and return it later. Type an address on the map, or tap "Use my current location", to see the nearest ones.

Can tourists rent a power bank in Estepona?

Yes. You rent through the operator's app or at the station and pay by card, so visitors can use them too. Return it to a station of the same operator before you leave.
