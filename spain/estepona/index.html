Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Estepona?

There are 32 power bank rental stations in Estepona, run by Revive and FireVolt. Stations are mostly in hotels, restaurants, bars and beach clubs. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Estepona?

The operators here show the price in their app or on the station before you start.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Estepona?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.

Where can I charge my phone in Estepona?

At any of the 32 power bank rental stations in Estepona: rent a portable charger, keep using your phone while it charges and return it later. Tap "Find stations near me" on the map to see the nearest ones.

Can tourists rent a power bank in Estepona?

Yes. You rent through the operator's app or at the station and pay by card, so visitors can use them too. Return it to a station of the same operator before you leave.
