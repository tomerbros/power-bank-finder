Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Málaga?

There are 30 power bank rental stations in Málaga, run by FireVolt and Revive. Stations are mostly in bars, restaurants and nightclubs. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Málaga?

The operators here show the price in their app or on the station before you start.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Málaga?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.

Where can I charge my phone in Málaga?

At any of the 30 power bank rental stations in Málaga: rent a portable charger, keep using your phone while it charges and return it later. Tap "Find stations near me" on the map to see the nearest ones.

Can tourists rent a power bank in Málaga?

Yes. You rent through the operator's app or at the station and pay by card, so visitors can use them too. Return it to a station of the same operator before you leave.
