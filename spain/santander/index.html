Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Santander?

There are 33 power bank rental stations in Santander, run by VolterGy and FireVolt. Stations are mostly in restaurants, clubs, cafés and campsites. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Santander?

From the published prices: VolterGy €3.50 for an hour, max €8.75 a day; FireVolt €3 for an hour, max €5 a day. The cheapest hour is €3 with FireVolt.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Santander?

For an hour, FireVolt at €3, going by the published prices; for a whole day, FireVolt with a maximum of €5.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Santander?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.

Where can I charge my phone in Santander?

At any of the 33 power bank rental stations in Santander: rent a portable charger, keep using your phone while it charges and return it later. Type an address on the map, or tap "Use my current location", to see the nearest ones.

Can tourists rent a power bank in Santander?

Yes. You rent through the operator's app or at the station and pay by card, so visitors can use them too. Return it to a station of the same operator before you leave.
