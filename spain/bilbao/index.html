Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Bilbao?

There are 21 power bank rental stations in Bilbao, run by FireVolt and voozaa. Stations are mostly in bars, restaurants and cafés. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Bilbao?

From the published prices: FireVolt €1.90–€6 for an hour, max €5–€30 a day; voozaa €2 for an hour, max €10 a day. The cheapest hour is €1.90 with FireVolt.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Bilbao?

For an hour, FireVolt at €1.90, going by the published prices; for a whole day, FireVolt with a maximum of €5.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Bilbao?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.

Where can I charge my phone in Bilbao?

At any of the 21 power bank rental stations in Bilbao: rent a portable charger, keep using your phone while it charges and return it later. Tap "Stations near me" on the map to see the nearest ones.

Can tourists rent a power bank in Bilbao?

Yes. You rent through the operator's app or at the station and pay by card, so visitors can use them too. Return it to a station of the same operator before you leave.
