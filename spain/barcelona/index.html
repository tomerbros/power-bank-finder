Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Barcelona?

There are 179 power bank rental stations in Barcelona, run by FireVolt, Buzz, Fully, ANYWATT and Revive. Stations are mostly in bars, restaurants, cafés and shops. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Barcelona?

Published prices: Buzz: €1.99 / 30 min · max €10 / day; Fully: €2–3 / hour. The other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you start.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Barcelona?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.

Where can I charge my phone in Barcelona?

At any of the 179 power bank rental stations in Barcelona: rent a portable charger, keep using your phone while it charges and return it later. Tap "Find stations near me" on the map to see the nearest ones.

Can tourists rent a power bank in Barcelona?

Yes. You rent through the operator's app or at the station and pay by card, so visitors can use them too. Return it to a station of the same operator before you leave.
