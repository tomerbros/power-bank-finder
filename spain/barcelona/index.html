Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Barcelona?

There are 267 power bank rental stations in Barcelona, run by Buzz, FireVolt, voozaa, Fully, ANYWATT, OneFlash and Revive. Stations are mostly in restaurants, bars, cafés and shops. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Barcelona?

From the published prices: Buzz €3.98 for an hour, max €10 a day; FireVolt €1.90–€6 for an hour, max €5–€30 a day; voozaa €2–€4 for an hour, max €10 a day; Fully €3 for an hour, max €12 a day. The cheapest hour is €1.90 with FireVolt. ANYWATT, OneFlash and Revive show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Barcelona?

For an hour, FireVolt at €1.90, going by the published prices; for a whole day, FireVolt with a maximum of €5.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Barcelona?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.

Where can I charge my phone in Barcelona?

At any of the 267 power bank rental stations in Barcelona: rent a portable charger, keep using your phone while it charges and return it later. Tap "Stations near me" on the map to see the nearest ones.

Can tourists rent a power bank in Barcelona?

Yes. You rent through the operator's app or at the station and pay by card, so visitors can use them too. Return it to a station of the same operator before you leave.
