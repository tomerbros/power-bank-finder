Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Madrid?

There are 97 power bank rental stations in Madrid, run by FireVolt, Revive and ANYWATT. Stations are mostly in restaurants, bars, nightclubs and sports clubs. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Madrid?

The operators here show the price in their app or on the station before you start.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Madrid?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.

Where can I charge my phone in Madrid?

At any of the 97 power bank rental stations in Madrid: rent a portable charger, keep using your phone while it charges and return it later. Tap "Find stations near me" on the map to see the nearest ones.

Can tourists rent a power bank in Madrid?

Yes. You rent through the operator's app or at the station and pay by card, so visitors can use them too. Return it to a station of the same operator before you leave.
