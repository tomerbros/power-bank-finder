Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Ibiza?

There are 124 power bank rental stations in Ibiza, run by Revive, Mobbi and FireVolt. Stations are mostly in hotels, restaurants, bars and beach clubs. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Ibiza?

Published prices: Mobbi: €1 / 30 min. The other operators show the price in their app or on the station before you start.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Ibiza?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.

Where can I charge my phone in Ibiza?

At any of the 124 power bank rental stations in Ibiza: rent a portable charger, keep using your phone while it charges and return it later. Tap "Find stations near me" on the map to see the nearest ones.

Can tourists rent a power bank in Ibiza?

Yes. You rent through the operator's app or at the station and pay by card, so visitors can use them too. Return it to a station of the same operator before you leave.
