Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Palma de Mallorca?

There are 88 power bank rental stations in Palma de Mallorca, run by Revive, FireVolt and voozaa. Stations are mostly in restaurants, bars, kiosks and clubs. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Palma de Mallorca?

From the published prices: FireVolt €1.90–€6 for an hour, max €5–€30 a day; voozaa €2–€4 for an hour, max €10 a day. The cheapest hour is €1.90 with FireVolt. Revive show the price in the app or on the station.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Palma de Mallorca?

For an hour, FireVolt at €1.90, going by the published prices; for a whole day, FireVolt with a maximum of €5.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Palma de Mallorca?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.

Where can I charge my phone in Palma de Mallorca?

At any of the 88 power bank rental stations in Palma de Mallorca: rent a portable charger, keep using your phone while it charges and return it later. Tap "Find stations near me" on the map to see the nearest ones.

Can tourists rent a power bank in Palma de Mallorca?

Yes. You rent through the operator's app or at the station and pay by card, so visitors can use them too. Return it to a station of the same operator before you leave.
