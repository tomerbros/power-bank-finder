Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Benalmádena?

There are 9 power bank rental stations in Benalmádena, run by FireVolt and Revive. Stations are mostly in restaurants, bars and sports clubs. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Benalmádena?

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Benalmádena?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.

Where can I charge my phone in Benalmádena?

At any of the 9 power bank rental stations in Benalmádena: rent a portable charger, keep using your phone while it charges and return it later. Tap "Find stations near me" on the map to see the nearest ones.

Can tourists rent a power bank in Benalmádena?

Yes. You rent through the operator's app or at the station and pay by card, so visitors can use them too. Return it to a station of the same operator before you leave.
