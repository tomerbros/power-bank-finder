Opening hours and status

1,132 of the 1,659 stations publish their opening hours; 543 stay open past midnight on at least one day; 43 are open around the clock. The map shows whether each one is open right now. On 22 November 2026, 1,097 stations reported themselves online and 350 offline or closed, and 212 don't report a status. On 22 November 2026, the 907 stations that publish their stock had 6,537 power banks ready to rent between them.

How power bank rental works in Spain

Find a station on the map, then rent a power bank with the operator's app or, at some stations, by tapping your bank card. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any station of the same operator. Each operator page explains how it works and what it costs.

Questions

How many power bank rental stations are there in Spain?

Powerbank Radar lists 1,659 stations from 7 operators in Spain (updated 22 November 2026), most of them in Valencia, Barcelona and Benidorm.

Which operator has the most stations in Spain?

FireVolt, with 1,048 of the 1,659 stations (63%).

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Spain?

Published prices: VolterGy: €1.75 / 30 min · max €8.75 / day; Buzz: €1.99 / 30 min · max €10 / day; Fully: €2–3 / hour; Mobbi: €1 / 30 min. Other operators show the price in their app or on the station.
