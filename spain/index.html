Opening hours and status

1,341 of the 1,980 stations publish their opening hours; 636 stay open past midnight on at least one day; 45 are open around the clock. The map shows whether each one is open right now. On 26 November 2026, 1,231 stations reported themselves online and 441 offline or closed, and 308 don't report a status. On 26 November 2026, the 1,068 stations that publish their stock had 7,748 power banks ready to rent between them.

How power bank rental works in Spain

Find a station on the map, then rent a power bank with the operator's app or, at some stations, by tapping your bank card. Charge your phone on the go and return the power bank to any station of the same operator. Each operator page explains how it works and what it costs.

Questions

How many power bank rental stations are there in Spain?

Powerbank Radar lists 1,980 stations from 11 operators in Spain (updated 26 November 2026), most of them in Barcelona, Valencia and Benidorm.

Which operator has the most stations in Spain?

FireVolt, with 1,068 of the 1,980 stations (54%).

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Spain?

From the published prices: FireVolt €1.90–€6 for an hour, max €2–€30 a day; voozaa €2–€4 for an hour, max €10 a day; Buzz €3.98 for an hour, max €10 a day; VolterGy €3.50 for an hour, max €8.75 a day; Fully €3 for an hour, max €12 a day; Mobbi €2–€4 for an hour; FLAPY €4 for an hour, max €10 a day. The cheapest hour is €1.90 with FireVolt. ANYWATT, Last Call, OneFlash and Revive show the price in the app or on the station.

What is the most I can pay for a day's power bank rental in Spain?

Most operators stop charging at a daily maximum. The lowest in Spain is €2 (FireVolt); see the table for each operator's.
