Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Murcia?

There are 8 power bank rental stations in Murcia, run by voozaa and FireVolt. Stations are mostly in bars, entertainment venues and event locations. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Murcia?

From the published prices: voozaa €2–€4 for an hour, max €10 a day; FireVolt €3 for an hour, max €9 a day. The cheapest hour is €2 with voozaa.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Murcia?

For an hour, voozaa at €2, going by the published prices; for a whole day, FireVolt with a maximum of €9.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Murcia?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.

Where can I charge my phone in Murcia?

At any of the 8 power bank rental stations in Murcia: rent a portable charger, keep using your phone while it charges and return it later. Tap "Find stations near me" on the map to see the nearest ones.

Can tourists rent a power bank in Murcia?

Yes. You rent through the operator's app or at the station and pay by card, so visitors can use them too. Return it to a station of the same operator before you leave.
