Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Castellón de la Plana?

There are 11 power bank rental stations in Castellón de la Plana, run by voozaa, FLAPY and FireVolt. Stations are mostly in bars, nightclubs and restaurants. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Castellón de la Plana?

From the published prices: voozaa €2–€4 for an hour, max €10 a day; FLAPY €4 for an hour, max €10 a day; FireVolt €3 for an hour, max €9 a day. The cheapest hour is €2 with voozaa.

Which is the cheapest power bank rental in Castellón de la Plana?

For an hour, voozaa at €2, going by the published prices; for a whole day, FireVolt with a maximum of €9.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Castellón de la Plana?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.

Where can I charge my phone in Castellón de la Plana?

At any of the 11 power bank rental stations in Castellón de la Plana: rent a portable charger, keep using your phone while it charges and return it later. Tap "Stations near me" on the map to see the nearest ones.

Can tourists rent a power bank in Castellón de la Plana?

Yes. You rent through the operator's app or at the station and pay by card, so visitors can use them too. Return it to a station of the same operator before you leave.
