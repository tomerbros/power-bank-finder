Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Seville?

There are 16 power bank rental stations in Seville, run by FireVolt. Stations are mostly in bars, restaurants, nightclubs and train stations. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Seville?

From the published prices: FireVolt €2–€6 for an hour, max €5–€30 a day. The cheapest hour is €2 with FireVolt.

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Seville?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.

Where can I charge my phone in Seville?

At any of the 16 power bank rental stations in Seville: rent a portable charger, keep using your phone while it charges and return it later. Tap "Stations near me" on the map to see the nearest ones.

Can tourists rent a power bank in Seville?

Yes. You rent through the operator's app or at the station and pay by card, so visitors can use them too. Return it to a station of the same operator before you leave.
