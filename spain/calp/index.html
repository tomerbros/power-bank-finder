Questions

Where can I rent a power bank in Calp?

There are 12 power bank rental stations in Calp, run by FireVolt and Revive. Stations are mostly in restaurants, bars, beach clubs and hotels. The map shows the nearest ones to you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank in Calp?

Can I return the power bank somewhere else in Calp?

Yes, at any station of the same operator. You can't return it to another operator's station.

Where can I charge my phone in Calp?

At any of the 12 power bank rental stations in Calp: rent a portable charger, keep using your phone while it charges and return it later. Tap "Find stations near me" on the map to see the nearest ones.

Can tourists rent a power bank in Calp?

Yes. You rent through the operator's app or at the station and pay by card, so visitors can use them too. Return it to a station of the same operator before you leave.
